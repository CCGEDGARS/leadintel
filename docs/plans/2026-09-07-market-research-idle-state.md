# Market Research Idle-State UX Fix

Approved behavior:
- All three research-mode buttons are equal width and equal height.
- Before explicit user activation, none of the three modes appears selected.
- The research review panel, planned searches, source discovery and research-specific details stay hidden until the user explicitly clicks a research mode.
- Company Brain context may remain visible before research, but must be clearly labeled as profile context rather than a market-research result.
- After a mode is clicked, only that mode is visually selected and its existing review workflow may proceed unchanged.
