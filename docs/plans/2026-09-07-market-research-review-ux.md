# Market Research Review UX refinement

Approved product behavior:
- keep three research modes as the entry point;
- review panel should present a compact, human-readable Research Plan instead of exposing long raw search queries by default;
- exact generated searches remain available behind an expandable `View exact searches` control;
- each mode should communicate depth and evidence capacity clearly;
- source discovery should transition from a working state to a clear completion state such as `8 relevant sources found` and retain per-source include/exclude controls;
- Market Intelligence should be differentiated by breadth of investigation and source-map depth, not merely by raw search count.
