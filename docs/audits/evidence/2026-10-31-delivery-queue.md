## Operational constraints and investigation

1. Preserve `sending`, ambiguous queue states, request-hash reservations and provider-acceptance receipts. An absent sent status is not proof that no submission occurred. Do not clear a hold or mint a fresh key to bypass uncertainty. There is no universal exactly-once guarantee.
2. Known undispatched/rejected transient attempts retain bounded backoff and at most three attempts. Ambiguous submission is never an automatic retry. A pre-reservation worker interruption can conservatively hold the workspace; review actual provider/reservation evidence rather than automatically expiring the claim.
3. Successful acceptance/table persistence and later CRM/source authorization are separate. CRM failures must not reopen the delivery. The actual default-CRM test proves local transaction behavior only; live provider correctness and real persistence remain unproven.
4. Acceptance recovery does not call a provider or confer sending permission. Follow-up repair creates deterministic existing-sequence steps under current policy; each eventual dispatch independently validates the original source/recipient and all current gates. Original source receipts are never rebound to newer Profile/Strategy/sender data.
5. Investigate using queue/sequence IDs, exact source and delivery reservation records, safe provider IDs, `outreach_automation.confirmation_persist_failed`, `acceptance_recovered`, `crm_sync_failed_after_send`, reply failure and follow-up creation audit events. Do not log raw provider responses or private message content to expand diagnostics.
6. Reply classification remains heuristic and schema-limited: bounce/DSN classification was not added in this scope. The exact sender of an unsubscribe is suppressed; an arbitrary different sender is not substituted for the original recipient. Reply polling is bounded, with no demonstrated production latency or alert SLA.
7. Historical source/identity-unbound queues remain review-required. A passing fixture, canonical source stamp, approval or saved draft does not activate automation. The production manual-only switch and separate activation/provider/paid-action permissions remain authoritative.
