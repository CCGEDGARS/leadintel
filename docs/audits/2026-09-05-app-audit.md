## Implemented
- Removed furniture-specific and Latvia-specific substitutions that changed unrelated companies' facts.
- Added authenticated, whole-field display translation for the profile editor. English interface stays separate from content language; Latvian is the default. Original saved data is preserved. Translation failures are visible and retryable.
- Translation responses require every requested field and unchanged numeric values. These checks do not prove flawless Latvian or factual equivalence; native-language and live-model review remain required.
- Draft framework content is no longer automatically labeled evidence-backed because a field is populated.
- Renamed commercial percentages as input/source coverage; completeness is not market strength or claim verification.
- Research reruns refresh unreviewed drafts while preserving reviewed/customer-entered answers and provenance metadata.
- Research detects answer/website edits made while a request runs and avoids overwriting them.
- Unified module versions to prevent duplicate engine/UI initialization and security-wrapper replacement.
- Kept full-width positioning layout and English field labels.
- Protected suppression at the central CRM transition boundary: archive and customer actions cannot silently reactivate a suppressed company; only an explicit owner restore can do so.
- Added CRM pagination, stale-response protection, and recoverable transport errors so lists beyond 100 records load safely and rapid selections cannot show the wrong company.
- Replaced truncated Gmail idempotency values with a SHA-256 digest of the complete domain, approval and recipient identity.
- Gmail delivery is recorded only when the durable server message status is explicitly `sent`; `sending` and `failed` states remain unconfirmed.
