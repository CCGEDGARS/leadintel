# Buyer contact buttons — owner-approved contract, 31 October 2026

This records the owner's explicit instruction: first perform the maximum supported bounded **free-source contact research for every retained buyer**, then use independent paid E-mail and Phone controls for missing confirmed data. It supersedes earlier descriptions in `buyer-quality-supervisor.md` which made the visible E-mail/Phone buttons combine free research with paid lookup. Research depth, identity, evidence, provider permissions, spending limits, company qualification and automatic-workflow gates remain authoritative.

## Sequence and controls

1. New company selection/discovery performs the existing dossier, official contact inventory, all-retained-person adaptive free checks, direct-email discovery, priority deep wave and three-query Gmail plan. Respect the existing ten-identity, source, query and deadline bounds. Preserve receipts, partial results, explicit resume and saved evidence. Never claim the whole internet was exhausted or that a missing contact does not exist.
2. **E-mail** is green only when an exact-person email passes the configured contact-confirmation/recipient policy. Otherwise its idle actionable state is white. Clicking it requests approved **paid email** lookup only, for that stable buyer identity. It never starts/refreshes free research or purchases phone data. A confirmed email is reused without another purchase.
3. **Phone** is green only for exact-person verified provider evidence or a retained named, attributed official/public business source with a successful source check within the existing 30-day reuse window. Otherwise its idle actionable state is white. Clicking it requests approved **paid phone** lookup only, independently of E-mail. It never starts free research or purchases email/profile research in the Treg phone-only path. An already confirmed number is reused without another purchase. A pending provider request is refreshed/reconciled, not blindly replayed.
4. **Recheck** explicitly refreshes that buyer's free-source checks and source reads. It cannot fall through to automatic paid contact lookup, even when paid permissions are ON. The default initial/public research path is likewise free-only. Existing explicitly authorized automatic execution remains a distinct caller/intent; neither saved preferences nor a Recheck authorizes it.
5. **Proceed** becomes green/available when the exact buyer has a confirmed usable email and the existing identity, qualification, session, synchronization and operation guards allow the handoff. Phone is optional. Proceed saves/selects the same buyer and address for Messages; it never authorizes sending.

## Confirmation and blocked states

- Patterns, generated addresses, Gmail guesses/possible matches and unrelated contacts never turn a control green.
- Provider-listed phone numbers remain visibly unconfirmed until attribution/verification supports confirmation. A provider return, a phone number string or a historical successful email is insufficient proof of phone ownership or reachability. A named published phone is a source-confirmed attribution, not a test call or reachability guarantee.
- Paid action requires the selected provider/service's existing permission, connection, readiness and limits. Paid-search ON is saved consent, not unlimited budget or evidence of dispatch. No provider switching, activation, budget increase, outbound message or real paid test is implied by this fix.
- Missing mandatory free checks block a paid action with a useful Recheck route. Company qualification failures route back to Companies. Do not silently substitute a free run for the requested paid action or waive company/identity gates.
- Busy/disabled controls may be gray; explain loading, saving, sync conflicts and qualification blocks. Confirmation remains visible and does not become an unconfirmed result merely because another operation is busy.
- Use stable person IDs plus current full identity, company/workspace/user and operation guards through dialogs, queues, provider requests, responses, persistence and downstream selection. Never replace a removed buyer with the person at the old card index.
