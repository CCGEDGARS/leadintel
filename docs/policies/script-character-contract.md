# Core-reference character contract — 7 November 2026

Owner instruction: use only real, reference-backed facts; the core template remains the reference; the adjusted message cannot exceed its character ceiling.

Every active Email, LinkedIn and Phone template has a numeric `maximumCharacters`, calculated from its own original body. An original is wording authority, not factual evidence. Where a legacy template has no separate original, its saved body is the reference. The server calculates the ceiling before stripping private originals from the public-to-workspace catalogue. Personal templates use their own reference. Each InMail sequence step uses its own saved stage body and never inherits the direct LinkedIn message ceiling. A revised master has its own ceiling; existing drafts/history are preserved.

NLP email has an explicit upper ceiling of **1,074 characters**, agreed from the uploaded NLP.docx. This excludes the heading “NLP STYLE”. The body includes spaces, blank lines, greeting, signature and full links. Subjects are separate and retain the 60-character ceiling. Count NFC-normalized Unicode code points, with CRLF/CR counted as one LF; HTML and highlighting markup do not count.

The editor and library show the limit. Oversized drafts remain editable and savable for recovery, but cannot pass final approval/flow/send checks or be copied for use through the channel controls. Assistant previews must fit before acceptance. Never truncate a name, URL or sentence to fit. Never change the master to make an invalid draft appear compliant.

Short noun-phrase fields accept only short factual phrases. Whole Profile answers and meeting agendas are not noun-phrase material; when they do not fit, retain the visible missing-field marker. Team experience is attributed to the team, not company age. Cookie duration, project timing and warranties do not establish experience. A missing/contradictory fact remains missing. Research receipts and source links remain available in the existing research panel.

Validation covers Unicode counts, all 15 channel/style combinations, catalogue original omission, oversized manual and assistant copies, team attribution, cookie durations, field shape, preserved originals, phone UI, and real browser/CRM save/reopen. These checks do not establish that every source assertion is true or that a private owner's historical draft has been repaired; production release proof and authenticated owner acceptance remain separate.
