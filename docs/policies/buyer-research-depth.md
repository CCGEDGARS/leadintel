# Mandatory Buyers research standard

Approved by Edgars on 20 October 2026. Applies to new and explicitly refreshed Step 5 research in manual and automatic flows. Runtime authority: `BUYER_CONTACT_RESEARCH_POLICY` and `runMandatoryBuyerResearch` in `customer/discovery-engine.js`.

## Optimal default: Adaptive deep

1. Read the official website and relevant staff, contact and leadership evidence before claiming confirmed contacts. Initial company acquisition remains bounded to eight pages and 35 seconds. Search missing buying functions using the existing opportunity/local-role coverage plan.
2. Research every retained identity, up to ten, in priority order. Two people run concurrently. Mandatory person checks cover the official domain, public business contact sources, phone, direct public LinkedIn evidence and a dedicated Gmail query using the person, company and the existing name-variant algorithm. Gmail findings require a published person–company–address association.
3. For up to four priority people still missing a published company email, execute one deeper wave of two person-and-company-specific searches, including PDFs, supplier/industry/association evidence and observed company email patterns. Read relevant discovered pages. An address pattern alone cannot become a confirmed contact.
4. Reuse successful same-identity checks for 30 days and reuse fully read source pages within the run. Explicit force refresh bypasses the search cache. Source ownership, employer, current role and exact person–email association gates remain mandatory. Never infer purchasing authority from an available address.
5. Public contact research has a hard 180-second ceiling, at most two selected result pages per search, a shared cache capped at 24 source pages, and one deeper wave. Parent cancellation/context guards remain active. Late provider results cannot overwrite a canceled/replaced workspace run. Preserve partial results and allow explicit resume.

## Tools

- Direct public reading is the first extraction attempt. Firecrawl is permitted for discovery and extraction. Configured Scrapling is the fallback after direct reading and Firecrawl cannot read a relevant source. Do not force an unused tool to run merely to fill the report.
- Grounded web search discovers and investigates gaps; optional independent evidence review is attributed separately. Restricted LinkedIn scraping is excluded.
- Apollo identity discovery may run only under its existing workspace discovery opt-in; it does not reveal emails/phones. Apollo email/phone reveals and Hunter checks require their separate approvals. Mandatory public research cannot activate them or send outreach. Automatic new paid contact enrichment must wait for completed mandatory public checks; already accepted saved/public contacts keep their existing flow eligibility.

## Accountability check

Every new run retains the policy version, start/completion times, checked people, official pages actually read, reused checks, deeper escalation count, gaps and stop reason. Each known tool retains attempted, succeeded, failed, skipped and result counts with a bounded reason. A successful zero-result request counts as executed; an unconfigured or unused provider does not. The extraction response and server audit retain direct/Firecrawl/Scrapling outcomes, including failed attempts before a successful fallback.

The check passes only when each retained identity has completed its required checks, the necessary deeper wave is complete or supported public email evidence makes it unnecessary, and an underlying official page has been read. Failed checks, missing official evidence, cancellation or budget exhaustion remain incomplete. Passing research coverage does not promise an email exists, prove mailbox delivery or establish buying authority. Existing public confirmation and green Select & proceed eligibility remain separate evidence gates.

Earlier saved runs display **not assessed** until explicitly refreshed; opening Buyers never launches a full upgrade search. Reports and provider counters survive workspace normalization and CRM snapshots. Use the same policy for other customers, industries, languages and company names.

Fresh selection handoff (21 October 2026): explicitly selecting a new, unresearched company starts identity discovery and the existing public-contact workflow once. Merely opening/reloading Buyers or reselecting retained/attempted research does not restart it. With no full identities, the quality action must start/retry identity discovery. Show an unstarted search as **Research not started**, not a zero-email outcome. Failed starts remain visible and retryable; contact-phase activity blocks overlapping identity refreshes.

## Gmail execution correction — 21 October 2026

The shared mandatory path generated Gmail name variants but replaced the dedicated variant query with a broad public-contact query. It then copied the public check status into the Gmail channel, allowing the UI to claim completed Gmail searches that had not run. The older dedicated query remained in a legacy path bypassed for known identities.

Policy `20261021-adaptive-depth-v2` requires an independent `gmail` check in the shared manual/automatic engine. Each retained person gets the dedicated query, independent status, attempt count, timestamp and failure reason. Successful earlier official/public checks can still be reused; a missing Gmail receipt must run. The Gmail field reads its own check, never aggregate/public completion. Earlier company accountability remains available with its tool counters and is labelled as an earlier policy; it cannot satisfy the new quality gate. Earlier AI receipts are invalidated by the policy change. Generated patterns remain guesses; only published, explicitly attributed business Gmail evidence can be retained, and never as a confirmed company-domain email. Regression coverage reproduces the bypass, false completion, failure propagation, cached missing check and exact-owner attribution.

## Mandatory direct-email discovery and complete Gmail research — 21 October 2026

User-approved policy `20261021-adaptive-depth-v3` adds a mandatory `direct_email` check for every retained full identity. After reading the official/public evidence, run two work-email searches: the official domain with original/transliterated full-name variants, then exact candidate work addresses. Learn observed address formats from all attributed official company staff, including first-name-only formats, rather than only the selected buyers. At most eight ranked work-email hypotheses are shown. A learned pattern remains a hypothesis; ownership/source confirmation and mailbox verification stay separate. The existing deeper PDF/industry pass for four unresolved priority people still applies.

Every retained full identity also receives three independent Gmail searches: exact full name/employer with Gmail/Googlemail, the existing bounded name-variant address search, and original/transliterated names with employer and business-contact/PDF/association sources. Read selected underlying sources under the shared page/time limits. Each check records its actual queries, individual outcomes, source-result counts, timestamps and failures. Reuse requires an exact successful query-plan match. Failed, canceled or unexecuted queries never count as complete. Resume remains available when the 180-second or 24-page budget is reached; complete means this bounded plan actually finished, not that every internet source was searched.

Keep source-backed possible business Gmail matches in `gmailMatches`, separately from attributable work emails and name-based `gmailCandidates`. Require the exact full name or its transliterated equivalent, company context or an official company source, an actual published address token and a safe source URL. Reject unrelated people, other contact cards, model summaries, generated-email prose and wrong-domain tokens. Show match basis, source and **Possible match · Unconfirmed**. Name-based Gmail patterns are separately expandable with **Name pattern · Unconfirmed**, no source-match claim and no mailbox-existence claim. Never manufacture a source-backed match to fill an empty result.

Both candidate types and per-query receipts survive workspace normalization and CRM snapshots. Neither can turn Find email green, qualify as a confirmed company email, unlock automatic outreach or authorize paid services. Existing explicit Apollo/Hunter approvals remain in force. These requirements apply to every customer, company and industry.


## Mandatory official company contacts — 21 October 2026

Policy `20261021-official-contacts-v4` adds a shared company-wide official contact inventory before individual contact checks in manual and automatic research. Discover staff/contact/leadership and investor/media/sustainability/supplier sections with three domain-scoped queries. Read up to twelve official pages with three concurrent reads and a 90-second deadline. Reuse fully read pages and successful nonempty same-policy inventories for at most thirty days; explicit refresh rereads them. Empty inventories are rechecked. The Firecrawl fallback requests a fresh extraction with a two-second render wait so dynamic staff cards can load within its existing request deadline. Search/read failures and the bounded stop reason remain visible. A completed bounded scan is not an exhaustive-internet claim. Opening a saved record does not silently rerun it; **Refresh official contacts** is available independently of buyer discovery.

The official website and its published company email domain can differ. Preserve explicitly published addresses on the official source as attributed listings, including departmental inboxes on a legacy company domain. This does not authorize an entire alternate domain or prove mailbox delivery. Keep shared channels, named listings, identity, phone and profile evidence separate from confirmed buyer emails, purchasing suitability and recipient eligibility. Existing exact-owner, employer, source and paid-service approval gates remain in force.

Handle role-before-name and name-before-role cards, including heading text split by direct HTML extraction. Stop at the next card, unrelated section or footer. An official page does not make an unrelated LinkedIn link or reception phone personal evidence. Preserve selected buyer identifiers and verified fields when merging a sparse inventory record. Store the inventory and actual query/read outcomes in workspace and CRM snapshots; restore an inventory even when no buyer has been selected. Cancellation, identity/workspace changes and newer local evidence must prevent stale writes.

Policy v4 preserves unfinished official URLs across partial runs and resumes them first. A supplied official contact/team page is read before broad discovery, with same-company URL validation. Display the actual source pages even for empty results. A time-limited scan must not restart the same front of the website and silently discard the unread tail.

## Company dossier first — 21 October 2026

The approved entry order is Company dossier → independent second-provider research → evidence reconciliation → selected buyers → individual contact enrichment. The shared contract is `customer/company-dossier.js`; server orchestration is `backend/src/company-dossier.js`. Both manual discovery and approved automatic runs execute this before selecting buyers. A manual contact refresh also acquires/resumes the dossier first. User Settings select the main researcher and optionally the secondary. Automatic main follows the workspace main engine; automatic second follows Gemini, Claude, OpenAI excluding the first. All three support actual citation-backed web search. The second receives the original company/seller objective and query families, never the first answer. A third replaces a failed or unsupported pass. Fewer than two configured providers fails before a paid research call.

Dossier extraction rereads underlying sources. Exact passages support every retained fact/person/channel; a model summary alone is insufficient. Staff announced in prose are supported. Explicit delivery authority and direct CEO reporting can support an area director's executive function without asserting procurement authority. Current, incoming and departed people retain their effective dates; conflicting undated roles require review. Published departmental addresses and supplier forms stay separate from personal mailbox confirmation.

Workspace-scoped D1 checkpoints retain successful passes and execution receipts. A same-context partial run resumes missing research; completed same-context dossiers can be reused for 24 hours without extending their original evidence timestamp. Changing the provider/model selection invalidates the server checkpoint. Explicit Refresh rereads both providers. The dossier and receipts also survive workspace normalization and CRM snapshots, including when no eligible buyer exists. Failed refreshes preserve earlier evidence and show the failed latest attempt. Profile/workspace changes and cancellation prevent stale writes. Dossier completion never replaces mandatory per-person direct-email/Gmail receipts, public-source confirmation or Apollo/Hunter consent.
