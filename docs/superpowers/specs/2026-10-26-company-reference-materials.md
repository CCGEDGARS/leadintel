# Company materials, references and success stories

Approved in the October 26 conversation: upload up to five PDFs and ten JPG/PNG images; automatically read, analyze and retain the originals and analysis per workspace; use relevant sources automatically in requested working-copy writing, particularly Storyselling. No manual activation. Processing, Ready and Needs attention states; retry and delete. Never invent results or force an irrelevant reference.

## Architecture and approaches
Browser-only extraction cannot preserve originals or support images across devices. Reusing the three book slots would consume the wrong allowance and apply method-library rules to commercial evidence. Use a separate authenticated D1 manifest and private R2 originals/analysis with the existing customer-owned AI providers. Upload tickets avoid frontend payload ceilings; minute scheduler processes durable jobs with leases. Files stay outside synchronized customer JSON. Existing browser documents remain readable and removable; new files use durable storage.

Automatic retrieval is workspace-scoped, bounded and relevance-ranked. User-supplied materials establish attributed seller claims only, never buyer need or independently verified outcomes. Core-template preparation is excluded: references guide requested Writing Assistant alternatives and original/rewrite generation. General Writing retains its isolation. Source filenames/locations are shown; incomplete reads are flagged. No upload, generation or style choice authorizes sending.

## Acceptance
Server-enforced five PDF / ten image limits, 15 MiB each; extension, MIME and signature validation; immutable originals before AI; deduplication; authenticated membership and ticket expiry; cross-workspace isolation; lease/retry safety; extraction of readable PDFs and vision processing of images/scanned PDFs; evidence-backed analysis; no Ready state for invalid output. Original download, reviewed text preview, deletion and retry supported. Race checks reject changed material during generation. Existing drafts, books and canonical templates remain intact.
