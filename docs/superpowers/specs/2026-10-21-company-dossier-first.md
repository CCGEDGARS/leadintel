# Company dossier first — approved design

User approval: 21 October 2026, integrate the discussed Company dossier → independent second-provider research → evidence reconciliation → selected buyers → individual contact enrichment flow.

A compact sourced dossier appears above buyers. The workspace chooses its main researcher (default existing active AI); a different connected provider performs independent research without seeing the first answer. The secondary can be overridden. The third provider replaces a failed provider, never the successful primary. At least two different providers must complete actual web research before dual research is complete. Missing configuration must fail before spending. All providers use existing customer-owned encrypted credentials and citation-backed web search.

Both passes use company, active seller offers, opportunity and internal role/query formulas. Read underlying sources; structured extraction must quote those sources. Reconciliation retains conflicts, dates, company routes and gaps and does not invent mailbox confirmation or purchasing authority. Dossier people enter existing qualification before top-four selection; existing mandatory per-person direct-email/Gmail checks and Apollo/Hunter approvals remain. Manual and approved automatic flows share server orchestration. Dossier, receipts, conflicts and sources survive workspace/CRM normalization and reload. No sending is authorized.

Freshness: reuse a completed same-context dossier for 24 hours; explicit buyer refresh rebuilds it. A failed refresh preserves the prior dossier but marks the latest attempt incomplete. Cancellation and workspace/profile/context changes block stale writes. Partial research never becomes dual-complete.

Acceptance: provider combinations, empty-search fallback, third-provider failover, citation and exact-quote rejection, role conflicts and effective dates, Stegra prose extraction, supplier route, cross-industry/name invariance, Settings persistence, manual/automatic order, save/reload, and existing confirmation gates. Fresh authenticated Stegra research and exact-SHA production proof are distinct release gates.
