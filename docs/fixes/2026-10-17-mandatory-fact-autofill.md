# Mandatory script fact autofill

The working NLP email could retain missing milestone, facility and opening-year markers even when its project evidence contained those details. The renderer received only a short selected-trigger excerpt. Full buyer source text and subsequent official research were outside that input, and the subject-research workflow could stop at 5/5 ready subjects without checking the body’s mandatory factual slots.

The renderer now considers the selected project’s full source paragraphs and relevant official project articles from the active dossier/research. It excludes unrelated projects and other domains. The selected trigger remains the anchor; research does not silently switch it to another opportunity. Supported schedule wording such as “scheduled to open in 2028” fills the existing opening-year slot. Protected originals and their paragraph frames remain unchanged.

The body’s missing factual atoms now participate in research planning, completion and retry visibility even when all subject lines are ready. Research is bounded and still uses the customer’s configured service. Missing facts are never guessed. A completed fact set may replace only an untouched, unsaved system draft that contains placeholders; saved/manual/approved drafts retain the existing reviewable update boundary. Evidence changes invalidate prepared-field fingerprints. Missing-public-fact guidance directs research rather than asking the user to type those facts manually.

Phone script choices and the Writing Assistant picker now fill available identity, project, service, company-description and experience values from active workspace context. Original patterns remain protected. Unknown identity/contact details stay unresolved; example-customer facts never become defaults. The assistant receives relevant buyer evidence as context.

Verification covers emitted NLP text through the real Update control, multi-paragraph source evidence, official-domain/project isolation, body-fact research despite 5/5 subjects, unchanged source originals, and personalized phone-picker loading. Release acceptance requires exact-SHA CI, backend health and production smoke proof separately.
