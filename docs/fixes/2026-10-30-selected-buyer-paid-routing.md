# Selected-buyer paid routing

The customer clicked Teemu's Email but saw activity on Klas. The screenshots include an October 28 saved Klas result, so they do not prove a new wrong-person charge.

Reproduced causes: manual email/phone preflight launched company-wide public research, whose completion automatically selected another top-ranked paid target. Controls and the deferred public callback also relied on mutable array indices. A scoped public job could be incorrectly reused for another selected buyer.

Bind clicked Email/Phone to a stable person ID. Re-resolve that identity in deferred confirmation; limit manual public checks to that ID and suppress automatic paid follow-up. Automatic paid selection is also blocked while a manual confirmation owns the company. Queue incompatible public scopes instead of reusing unrelated results. Rebind live person objects across asynchronous preparation, reject changed identities, and reject mismatched paid response IDs/domains before acceptance. Progress distinguishes the selected public scope from other buyers' retained data.

Regression tests exercise reorder between render/click and deferred confirmation, automatic top-buyer selection during manual preflight, changed identities and wrong-person responses. No live paid lookup was executed, no real charge/receipt was attributed to this bug, and no historical contact or approval was rewritten. Existing provider budgets, consent, evidence gates and sending controls remain intact.
