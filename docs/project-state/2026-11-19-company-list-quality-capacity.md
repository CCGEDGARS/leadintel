# Active-signal lists and 200-company capacity

Owner scope: prepare the application to build up to 200 properly researched companies; do not run a real 200-company search now. Only the active signal should drive a new list. The owner's current choice is Lookalike. Quantity changes volume, never the verification standard. Existing Companies → Buyers → Messages and saved search copies remain available.

## Root causes and changes

- Company-list options previously injected Lookalike and offered inactive signals, and discarded string-valued signal keywords. Options now derive from active, nondeleted Strategy signals, recognize a named Lookalike signal, and retain its reference semantics. An active reference ICP can supply the Lookalike lens. Legacy reference-only contexts without signal configuration remain supported; explicitly switched-off signals stay off. HTTP start resolves the current authorized workspace context again. Frozen tasks keep their reviewed criteria; new tasks use the active criteria.
- A valid quotation could count as a match without a separate check of what the quotation meant. New lists use policy `20261119-reviewed-evidence-v2`. Actual page reads, official identity, operating geography, selected-signal evidence and sourced commercial fit feed a separate reasoning review of attribution, comparison, commercial relevance and exclusions. Only a passing review counts. Lookalike uses the selected reference's quoted business traits and retained reference URL; it does not require an unrelated recent hiring/funding event. A review can require linked follow-up evidence or reject a candidate. This is an additional review by the configured reasoning provider, not a claim that another provider or independent publisher researched the company.
- Follow-ups previously reconstructed page text from retained quotes and could add date strings. New-policy follow-ups and review use actual saved page text and links. No unsupported URL is followed. Older jobs retain their policy and saved partial matches; they are not retroactively labelled reviewed.
- The fixed 500-operation ceiling and stop-on-first-error behavior made larger runs unnecessarily dependent on manual intervention. The reviewed allowance can now be 10–5,000 operations. The UI suggests 20 operations per requested company, at least 100, so a 200-company target shows 4,000. This is a maximum operation allowance, not a cost estimate, credit balance or guaranteed number of companies. It includes attempted searches, reads, assessment and review; underlying providers can bill multiple requests. Actual limits are enforced server-side. Resuming a failure uses the remaining allowance; only an exhausted allowance grants the explicitly displayed additional amount.
- Transient failures retry at later scheduler wakes, after 30 and 120 seconds, with a maximum of three attempts. Unreadable pages stay in incomplete checks and the run continues to other sources. Credit/authentication failures stop immediately with an actionable blocker. Insufficient credit in an extractor trace survives fallback error wrapping. A 429 alone is not labelled exhausted credits. Queries, rounds, follow-up depth, receipts, gap history and candidate URLs remain bounded. Repeated empty discovery and the finite plan end as incomplete, never a fabricated full list.
- HTTP wakes fit inside a 25-second background slice; scheduler slices remain 45 seconds. Each started operation and completed stage is checkpointed, every call is deadline bounded, and interrupted work is distinguished from completed work. Existing session, role, workspace, lease, revision, parent-task and shared invocation limits remain in force. Closing a browser does not revoke its session; signing out/revoking access still stops research.
- The top 10 appear first, with Show all and full-list export. Mobile rows become readable cards. Evidence, reference sources, review reasons and commercial hypotheses accompany exports and new CRM records. Existing CRM edits/research are preserved. Research history retains aggregate attempt outcomes plus recent receipts and durable per-company read/assessment/review receipts; earlier unrecorded operations are never reconstructed as successes.

## Acceptance evidence and limits

Pure and real-SQL tests exercise active-only selection, aliases, string keywords, same quality policy at 1/10/50/100/200, missing commercial evidence, failed review checks, attempted-operation accounting, allowance boundaries, automatic delayed recovery, wrapped credit errors, malformed responses, linked follow-ups retaining original source context, rejection/shortfall, cancellation/revocation, quota recovery, isolation, and CRM preservation.

The native Chromium journey enters through authenticated HTTP, builds 200 while the browser is closed using the registered `app.scheduled` wrapper, reopens in a fresh browser, switches between top 10 and all 200, exports CSV/Excel, saves every CRM batch, verifies desktop/mobile layout and enters Buyers explicitly. The saved-search and reviewed-chat task journeys also cover reopening, blocker recovery, exports and downstream handoff. Controlled services make these deterministic capacity and lifecycle checks; no real prospect research or paid contact lookup is invoked.

The controlled 200-result fixture used 623 successful operations within one reviewed 4,000-operation allowance. That fixture is not a prediction of real-world duration, cost, yield or factual accuracy. Live results depend on the selected references, available public evidence, connected provider health, configured limits and queue load. A real customer 200-company outcome was deliberately not requested. Exact-SHA deployment proof and authenticated customer research yield remain separate gates.

## Preservation and rollback

No data migration, storage/billing redesign, outreach/template change or automatic CRM write is introduced. Existing list and CRM rows remain owned by their current tables. Old results retain their old evidence status. A rollback can restore the prior runner/UI while retaining the saved JSON; new review fields are additive. Prior snapshots, paid-contact approval, buyer qualification and sending gates remain separate. Future fixes must test through HTTP, the registered scheduler and browser reopening instead of treating provider configuration or a mocked list count as customer research proof.
