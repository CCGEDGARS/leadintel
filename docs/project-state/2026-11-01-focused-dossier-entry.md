While research resumes, the dossier banner reports in progress rather than displaying the previous attempt as a current failure. Saved evidence and per-query resume retain their existing protections.

Validation includes cross-industry context isolation, every selected role retained in objectives, explicit 30-formula preservation, two providers completing six source-backed queries each, manual/automatic entry parity and existing lifecycle/approval suites.
