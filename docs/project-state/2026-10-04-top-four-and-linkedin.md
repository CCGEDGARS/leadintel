# Top-four research and LinkedIn message handoff — 4 October 2026

## Authorized behavior

Discover up to ten names and actual job titles. Research the four highest-priority eligible buyers by default, rank researched recommendations by highest qualification score, and show the remaining candidates as compact disclosures. Relevant function, title-inferred decision responsibility, identity/employer support, sources and freshness determine priority; an easy email does not add ranking points. Actual purchasing authority remains unconfirmed without evidence.

Users can research an individual candidate or the remaining candidates. Evidence-disqualified picks receive bounded replacement research from the remaining ten. Provider failures do not trigger another automatic batch. Historical evidence for other buyers is preserved rather than erased to make the display appear newly researched.

Gmail pattern candidates are generated and included in the person-and-company public search. They remain explicitly guessed and unconfirmed, outside sourced contact findings and automatic outreach. A public result must connect the person, address and employer context; mailbox deliverability does not establish ownership. Public email/phone source links, qualification holds and research diagnostics persist in the ten-person CRM snapshot.

Inside Confirm LinkedIn, “Confirm LinkedIn & create message” saves the exact reviewed profile and buyer selection, opens Messages and generates an editable LinkedIn direct-message draft. Email confirmation is not required for this channel. Workspace/profile substitution, qualification holds, suppressed companies and failed persistence block the handoff. Saved drafts retain edits on reopening. LinkedIn drafts cannot produce an approved email send payload. No sending is authorized.

## Root causes corrected

The existing contact follow-up used every candidate, and function diversity could reserve a weaker recommendation. Scope detailed research to selected IDs, merge results back into the complete owner record before CRM persistence, preserve unrelated conflicts/evidence, and make score order authoritative. Empty normalized role arrays must not mask active workspace roles; legacy unresolved identities still receive bounded acquisition while remaining held.

LinkedIn confirmation previously ended at a review record. Add an explicit channel-specific handoff, persist channel and selected identity, and independently validate the channel in content creation. Messages navigation accepts either an accepted company-email contact or an exact confirmed LinkedIn profile.

## Validation

1,655 customer tests passed, zero failures. New behavioral checks cover default four versus untouched six, one-person expansion, evidence-disqualification replacement, CRM snapshot/reload, Gmail guess/evidence separation, highest-score and cross-industry/name invariance, exact LinkedIn profile/workspace gates, no-email draft generation, edit preservation and email-delivery exclusion.

Two additional production smoke checks validate selective research assets and the LinkedIn handoff assets. Exact production proof and authenticated customer observations are recorded separately in the final handover; tests alone do not prove customer acceptance.
