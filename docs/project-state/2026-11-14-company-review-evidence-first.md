# Explicit company review: evidence ownership and bounded yield

Acceptance outcome: a saved-company review uses valid retained evidence before web calls, completes company checks progressively, preserves earlier completed companies and the unfinished queue, and survives cloud save/reopen and the Buyers handoff. A requested five-company target is never permission to fabricate qualification or silently enlarge the saved provider allowance.

## Observations and controlled reproductions

The authenticated production workspace retained31 domain records but its last11-request review finished zero checks. The saved export contains496 raw evidence rows; all31 company records have readable official evidence, and18 have additional official raw-page URLs. These aggregate facts do not establish current headcount, current purchasing fit, or qualification. Some apparent headcount statements are historical; do not refresh their dates or bless them through parsing alone.

The explicit recheck path sets targetOnly and bypasses normal discovery's saved-evidence-first queue. A failing actual-run test spent four web operations to recheck a company whose fresh official operating evidence could be reassessed without web calls. A second failing case used its three-request allowance rediscovering five known targets and finished none. Recheck now evaluates retained official company pages, reassesses current purchasing fit, then researches only the missing criterion, one company at a time. Failed AI assessments preserve their actual failure and do not trigger repurchase of fresh web evidence.

The expanded tests caught a related ownership defect: researchPool's progress callback replaced runCandidates with the latest fit batch. A five-company progressive review retained only the last company. Progress now replaces only the evaluated identities and preserves/ranks earlier completed companies. Existing mixed-score/ranking, failed-retry identity, cancellation and queue-rotation tests retain their behavioral assertions; web-specific observations now use the actual AI reassessment receipts where no web call is necessary.

A historical needsRecheck flag also forced web requests after a successful current-context assessment had already passed every manual qualification gate. The explicit review clears that flag only after the current full assessment is eligible, then replans before spending. A failing-before case used three requests despite fresh evidence; it now uses zero. Expired sources or missing proof still fail the unchanged assessment.

The explicit company-size query incorrectly required target-country text on the same page as company-wide headcount. A company's overall employee floor and its target-market presence are different facts. Headcount queries now search official company facts without requiring a country term; the unchanged market-presence qualification gate remains separate.

Official headcount extraction also missed explicit first-person team statements and space-grouped thousands. Regression cases accept attributed factual counts, preserve the original source date, and continue holding conflicting counts, customer counts, recruitment plans and ranges that cross the floor. This grammar correction alone does not establish the native workspace's current headcount.

## Data and rollback

No schema migration, deletion, permission change, research auto-activation or contact purchase. Existing record identities, manual buyer roles, selected buyers and CRM records stay owned by their existing workspace. Additional raw evidence is reused only for the same official domain and an already-read source URL absent from the company's retained evidence; it cannot overwrite an existing source body or invent a new read time. Existing freshness, hard exclusions, purchasing-fit proof and source-quality gates remain authoritative. Rollback can remove the progressive review path while retaining all saved evidence and successful results; never clear business records to reset the flow.

## Verification boundaries

Controlled tests prove the execution ordering, zero-web reassessment, progressive bounded results, accumulated ranking and retained state. Mandatory CI must also execute the real authenticated HTTP/SQL/browser review and fresh-browser journeys. Exact-release PROVEN evidence and actual customer qualification remain separate acceptance gates. Record final deployed and authenticated results in the associated PR; do not substitute these controlled fixtures for real prospects.
