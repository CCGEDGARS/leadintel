Buyer qualification is a transparent evidence-priority score, not purchase probability or confirmed authority: opportunity-role fit 35, title-inferred responsibility 15, supported full identity 15, employer attribution 15, identity source quality 10, dated identity evidence freshness 10. Undated identity evidence gets zero freshness points. Former roles, abbreviated identities, wrong employers, missing sources, changed committees and identity conflicts fail eligibility. Verified emails cannot override these gates. Strongest-four selection includes function diversity within one ten-person shortlist. Contact readiness remains a separate verified company-email gate.

The full breakdown, supporting identity URL/date, uncovered-function research and email-verification outcome are kept in workflow state and the dedicated CRM intelligence snapshot. Each contact confirmation records verified / not_verified / error, timestamp and reason, rather than losing the reason in a transient toast. Existing CRM identity deduplication and workspace isolation are preserved.
