# Buyer actions and automatic free research

Approved in the 29 October conversation: E-mail → Phone → Recheck → Proceed. Replace the LinkedIn action with Recheck; retain LinkedIn inside the contact profile. Remove the separate Buyer Agent panel while keeping its identity, employer, role and opportunity checks in the research engine.

Buyers entry now resumes missing/stale public checks automatically, once per workspace/company per opening. Fresh completed checks are reused. Saved evidence renders immediately; new findings stream through the existing bounded research engine. Opening unresearched selections starts identity discovery. The earlier policies forbidding any automatic saved-record resume are superseded for this entry behavior. Recheck forces a person-scoped public refresh without buying contact data in manual mode; paid evidence and actual execution receipts remain retained.

E-mail and Phone select independent Treg channels. The current primary-provider controls are preserved: Treg uses independently approved email and phone channels; legacy provider paths retain their existing separate capability and consent requirements. Phone starts with mandatory public checks before any paid request. Paid limits, Setup activation, provider approvals, pending-request reuse and workspace/identity guards remain enforced. Automatic phone follows the independent Setup switch; saving preferences never starts lookup or sending by itself.

Proceed requires a qualified contact with a confirmed person-owned company or personal mailbox. Verified personal addresses must carry explicit personal email type, the exact external person ID and a named provider. Treg only accepts a personal provider mailbox when returned full-name ownership and independent deliverability verification both succeed. Wrong company work addresses, conflicting identities, shared inboxes and guesses remain ineligible. Messages and automation handoffs use the shared policy rather than rejecting every non-company-domain address.

Verification covers four action labels and gating, forced public recheck, saved free-research resume/reuse, exact personal ownership, manual channel isolation, both automatic phone-switch states, provider fallback, CRM handoff and buyer regressions. Production release is a separate unproven state until the exact-SHA release-integrity chain is satisfied.
