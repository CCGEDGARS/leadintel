# Companies recovery after a saved search

The reported Companies screen showed eight saved results, six previous scores, two awaiting scores, one earlier Buyers selection, and zero currently checked companies. It recommended finding companies while both search and evidence review were blocked. Its page check called for market research in Profile while its review button opened Strategy.

## Root causes and recovery

- Confirming unchanged targeting could rewrite reviewed answers from user to accepted. Opening an empty reference library could persist an empty portfolio in a different shape. Either representation could change the research signature. Comparison now recognizes equivalent saved inputs, and preserves holds when substantive inputs differ. Reapproving an unchanged Profile preserves manually edited Strategy criteria.
- Companies previously chose its next action from the number of results, independently of the checks that gate research. The guide, page check and batch review now resolve the applicable prerequisite. Genuine stale research opens the Profile market research workbench. Unapproved Strategy with current inputs opens Strategy. Once eligible, pending companies are reviewed from the saved queue. Navigation alone never starts a provider request.
- A text snippet from a company domain could increase the website-check counter. New runs count attributable page content, including page content returned with a search. Historical reports retain their recorded metric. Acquisition also stops when the required purchasing-fit provider is unavailable for the run, keeps the evidence obtained and reports the provider issue.

## Verification boundary

The controlled acceptance case uses the authenticated Companies interface, real HTTP and SQLite persistence to reopen saved results, follow the relevant actions, and check the retained Buyers selection. Separate runner tests exercise provider failures, web-request reuse and evidence of a company's operating market. Earlier scores remain historical until their supporting evidence passes review for the active criteria. The screenshots do not establish a cause for a particular company's score or a Firecrawl credit total.
