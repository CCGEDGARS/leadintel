# Contact confirmation policy and optional provider capabilities

This change applies across workspaces and company searches. The old Buyers approval used one consent flag for email and phone, and downstream paths accepted only a `verified` email. This prevented a source-confirmed company email from advancing without paid enrichment and did not distinguish optional capabilities sufficiently.

Apollo remains one account/connection. Its Settings card contains free people discovery, separate email and phone enrichment permissions, daily/monthly credit limits and an account link. Email and phone default off, including previously connected keys without explicit flags. Connecting, replacing or refreshing a key does not enable paid capabilities. Owner settings update permissions and limits together. Credit-consuming endpoints enforce the requested capability on the server; the existing CRM reservation and webhook controls still apply. Public LinkedIn URLs are identity evidence, not authenticated LinkedIn access.

Hunter remains in its own Settings card, off by default. It is never an Apollo setting. Enabling additional Hunter verification retains the separate delivery check and can consume Hunter credits. No provider guarantees 100% accuracy.

Workflow setup selects either public confirmation or provider verification and separately approves automatic Apollo email fallback and phone enrichment. A new workflow defaults to public confirmation. Existing Buyers configurations without a level retain provider verification, and old approval hashes require renewed review. Existing standalone automatic delivery approvals also retain provider verification until an explicit workflow configuration is saved.

Public confirmation requires an exact full name, individual company email and HTTPS official company source. The server retrieves the page and validates actual person–email attribution before persisting `public_confirmed`, the source URL, check timestamp and CRM activity. Guesses, generic inboxes, third-party listings, redirected sources, missing names and conflicting identities do not qualify. Public confirmation expires after 30 days and can be rechecked. It confirms ownership evidence, not mailbox deliverability; only an actual provider result receives provider verification.

Manual Buyers has independent Save buyer and Save & proceed actions. Incomplete contacts can be retained without opening Messages. Save & proceed checks the selected level, confirms an official source on the server when needed, rereads CRM and saves the selection before handing off. Confirm email requests Apollo, followed by Hunter only when enabled. Confirm phone has separate Apollo authorisation. LinkedIn remains public-profile review/manual confirmation.

Automatic Buyers, message handoff, queue creation and pre-send verification apply the selected confirmation policy and workspace/company isolation. Public confirmation cannot override suppression, sender connection, approved content, approved workflow revision or delivery permissions. Message creation does not authorise sending. Actual delivery still needs a connected mailbox and explicit delivery approval.

Validation uses SQLite CRM save/reload/activity tests, injected official-page evidence, provider-disabled/no-reservation tests, wrong-person/source/redirect/expiry/isolation checks, capability/credit-limit persistence and provider-free workflow approval. Existing regression fixtures now explicitly opt into enrichment when testing paid paths. No production provider credits or email sending are used in validation. Exact production proof is generated only after required CI, live manifest, backend health and all mandatory smoke checks succeed.

Authenticated settings validation exposed a pre-existing reporting gap: credit totals counted only legacy opportunity requests and the health caption identified customer-owned Apollo as platform-managed. The follow-up unifies reservations from both legacy and CRM ledgers for Settings and both budget gates, preserves workspace/date/cancelled/failed filtering, and labels the actual credential source and reservations. Pending phone reservations remain included.
