# Approved protected cores, AI Original and My Templates

User-approved design: four core styles (Professional, NLP, Friendly, Brutal Honesty) remain read-only. Every generated message is editable. AI Original chooses structure freely from reviewed research and approved seller facts, assesses opportunities to earn more, save costs and simplify work, and ends with a 30-minute Zoom invitation and Calendly action. It returns exactly five distinct factual subject options per draft, with user selection and direct subject editing. Weak evidence produces an honest simple introduction. No sending is authorized.

My Templates holds five named numbered slots. Users can save an edited message, copy a core, edit/rename a saved slot, use it for a fresh recipient, or explicitly replace an occupied slot. Save as template first replaces known identities, current project, website, Calendly and fit score with fields, asks AI to prepare a reusable pattern, and requires human review before storage. If AI fails, local conversion remains visible and manual review is required. AI preparation never overwrites the actual draft. Cancel discards unsaved review changes and aborts preparation. A response after cancellation, mode change, buyer handoff or workspace change cannot overwrite the review.

Names, companies, projects, scores, dates, links and sender proof must be reusable fields. User review includes any remaining literal facts. During reuse the personal pattern supplies structure only, not evidence: generation uses current workspace and recipient facts, omits unsupported claims and rejects unapproved URLs. Fit subject fields use the actual current company score and are enforced after generation. Never confuse fit with probability, buyer rank or opportunity score.

Version 6 migration moves real edits to personal slots once while historical untouched master bodies and chosen approved subjects consume no slots. Protected core subject selection persists separately. Existing custom data survives. Core restore does not delete personal work. Deletion is reversible until the slot is replaced; a tombstone prevents legacy remigration from resurrecting deleted records. Undo restores content and name. Unknown and out-of-range slots are rejected; implicit overwrite is rejected. Each slot is an atomic synchronization unit, so competing edits do not combine mismatched subject/body/name. Independent slots can merge.

Only canonical settings, subject choices and slot records persist in message-studio state; derived core/template arrays are omitted to prevent synchronization conflicts and duplicate payload growth.
