# Buyer readiness and decision priority

Confirmed company emails retain the green Select & proceed affordance during a workspace sync conflict. The card explicitly states that email readiness is established but the conflict must be resolved before saving the selection. Selection and Continue handlers still refuse conflicted state; no local or remote version is overwritten.

The previous title buckets gave company executives and procurement heads the same maximum authority score. Model v4 separates company executives, business-area executives, purchasing leaders, project directors, senior managers and managers. CEO Staff/office/support titles no longer qualify as company executives. Title authority remains inferred, not confirmed budget authority.

Priority components total 100: role fit 20, authority 25, identity 10, employer 10, source 10, freshness 10, named opportunity responsibility 15. Equivalent supported evidence can still tie. Contact availability is independent of priority.

Public buyer research now captures dated official text naming the buyer and linking their responsibility to the active opportunity. It preserves source URL, date and quotation through normalization and CRM snapshots. Existing verified signal evidence can support the same assessment. A recent official statement of purchasing/supplier-selection responsibility contributes 15; project responsibility contributes 12. Generic company signals, different people, adjacent unrelated sentences, generated summaries, third-party sources, stale/future evidence and former responsibility do not qualify. Actual purchase/budget authority remains explicitly unconfirmed.

Tests cover seniority differentiation, CEO staff, attributable evidence, negative attribution/freshness cases, cross-industry context, reload and CRM preservation, and green email readiness with blocked conflicted selection. Existing recipient identity and single-selection handoff regressions remain in place.
