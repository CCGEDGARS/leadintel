# Visible company search progress

The previous Companies view repeated an identifying-companies label without a visible meter or live elapsed time. Query searches already refreshed every two seconds, but identification requests did not refresh the view while waiting and treated all evidence batches as one check.

The search controls now have a prominent progress panel with a five-stage map, actual current-stage check counts, an elapsed clock, identified/checked/qualified counts and an accessible Cancel action. Search start brings the panel below the sticky header once; subsequent updates retain its position. Adaptive broadening explicitly revisits the source-search stage. Determinate percentages apply only to actual completed checks within a stage. Identification batches remain indeterminate until responses return; time alone never advances counts or qualification. The animated segment stays visible, and reduced-motion preferences stop its animation.

The lightweight UI clock updates its elapsed node without replacing the meter or Cancel button. It stops on completion, cancellation, scope replacement and page exit. Terminal incomplete, canceled and interrupted states preserve the saved results and do not show a successful 100% meter. Reopened historical runs use recorded timing rather than fabricating a completion time. Existing call budgets, targeting, qualifications, saved selections and provider dispatch are unchanged; identification only reports its actual settled batch counts. Company discovery remains dependent on the open tab and does not claim durable background execution.

Acceptance holds an actual authenticated company-extraction HTTP response after the controlled backend answers. Chromium verifies the visible indeterminate bar, unchanged batch counts as elapsed time advances, desktop/mobile layout, reduced motion, real Cancel, stopped timing and canceled-state restore after reopening. Existing market sources/report and all qualification gates remain intact. No additional production research is needed to verify the presentation.
