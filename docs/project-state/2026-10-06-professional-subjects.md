# Professional subject choices

Professional style now offers five immutable subject patterns: personal relevance, specific development, business benefit, curiosity, and direct introduction (`{{sender}}. {{company}}`). The selector is exclusive to Professional. Selecting saves a personal workspace subject without changing its message body; existing custom subjects remain intact until a choice is made. Manual subject edits clear the preset indication. Restore original removes the preset selection. Choice survives normalization and CRM snapshot persistence.

Generation uses the selected pattern, approved seller context and reviewed buyer evidence. Missing development or benefit evidence falls back to a practical idea for the buyer company. Do not invent a surname, project, result or benefit; translate subject wording for the selected language and preserve proper names. Other styles retain their subjects. Existing generated drafts are invalidated when a choice changes; outreach sending remains a separate action.

Validation: four new subject regression tests cover immutable options, save/reload, prompt propagation, personal edits, restore, style isolation and factual instructions. Customer suite: 1,759 cases; build and diff hygiene checked. Production requires exact-SHA release proof.
