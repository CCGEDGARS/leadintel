# Executive representation in the four priority buyers

User approved: reserve one of four places for a qualified current executive; other three are the highest-priority qualified buyers; all four stay sorted by score. If no executive qualifies, temporarily use the next qualified buyer and show an explicit gap. No outreach sending authorized.

Root cause: opportunity role expansion and a simple first-four slice did not preserve executive coverage. Executive discovery was not mandatory, and ten-person truncation could remove a lower-scored leader before top-four selection.

Implementation: a shared executive role group is included in research, identity parsing, qualification, coverage and directory discovery. Official executive evidence is queried first, with CEO/COO/division leadership, local-language variants and small-company owner/managing-partner support. Functional chiefs do not silently replace the company/division leadership search. Qualified executive representation survives ten-person truncation and selects one reserved place, with score-sorted output. Missing, stale, conflicting, wrong-employer and unsupported executive identities cannot fill the slot. Completed contact checks do not affect executive priority. Existing saved companies get a targeted three-query executive search with identity fallback, preserved buyer evidence and contact research only for the selected new executive. New searches apply the rule through the normal pipeline. Executive diagnostics persist in CRM; selection is recomputed after restore.

Validation: regressions cover an executive below the ten highest scores, exactly one reserved place, no fabricated executive, score sorting, cross-industry/name invariance, local aliases, directory titles, small-company ownership, targeted live-runtime preservation and normalized executive diagnostics. Full customer suite, build and exact-SHA production proof are required before production claims. Authenticated LKAB results must be inspected separately.

Authenticated production QA exposed two additional causes: an executive-heavy result pool could occupy three of the four places, and official management pages with generic titles were rejected despite explicit current named employment statements. The priority policy now selects exactly one supported executive plus up to three non-executive functional buyers. The reserved executive needs official employer evidence, authenticated directory identity or dated identity evidence; undated third-party executive profiles remain outside that reserved place. Official management statements are parsed only on the target domain, and up to two discovered leadership pages are read by the targeted follow-up. Existing ordinary-buyer eligibility and contact evidence stay intact. Additional regressions cover executive-heavy ranking and generic official management statements.
