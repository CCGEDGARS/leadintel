# Buyers guided continuation

The Buyers footer uses the same 48px minimum height, 10px corners and 14px text as the Messages primary action. It remains actionable during incomplete research and opens, highlights and focuses the next mandatory requirement. After source facts, role verification, the buyer quality requirements and recipient confirmation pass, it continues with the exact saved recipient. Per-card Proceed saves the recipient for this footer rather than bypassing the checks.

Active research displays its current stage separately from completed stages. The fifth active stage represents four completed stages rather than 100% completion. Failed source acquisition settles the loading state without running subsequent final review. Reloaded interrupted work becomes resumable error state, preserving previously found people and evidence. Missing facts are described as pending research rather than verified evidence.

Paid lookup limits and approvals remain enforced. A paused paid lookup explains the Setup recovery and public-source Recheck option; it does not increase limits or grant approvals. Tests use isolated fixtures and do not establish that the customer's research has completed or that their contacts are verified.
