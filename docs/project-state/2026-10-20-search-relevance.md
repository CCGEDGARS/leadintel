# Company-search relevance — 20 October 2026

## Outcome and causes

The approved change improves acquisition rather than inflating company scores. The deterministic fallback collapsed the buyer description to three keywords and injected fixed manufacturing/factory query families. A legacy reference-search wrapper then relabeled operational queries as signals and could restore generic manufacturing terms. Text-only company extraction missed operating service firms. Verification began in extraction order, follow-up reused generic queries, and one failed purchasing assessment vetoed healthy-domain follow-up.

## Implementation

- The shared planner derives operational need, reference similarity and active-signal families from the current effective targeting profile. Explicit signals/lookalike priorities and paused references remain authoritative. No-reference/no-signal searches use the confirmed buyer context. Seller service names remain offer metadata rather than supplier-search keywords. Queries fit the actual 600-character provider boundary.
- The installed reference integration delegates query acquisition to the new planner while retaining its evidence-comparison functionality. The existing authenticated planner accepts the operational-need route and explicitly reasons about purchasing relationships, native-language searches and scoped selection examples.
- Up to 60 inexpensive extracted names can be screened using sentences attributed to each company; the paid company-verification ceiling remains 30 and all browser paid calls retain their shared request budget. Uncertain fit is deprioritized, not called a rejection. Explicit non-prospect names, legal-name duplicates, seller/reference identities and exact rejected examples are screened before paid resolution. Distinct businesses sharing a name token remain separate.
- The runner researches deferred extracted names before another paid market-search wave, retains previous checks in diagnostics, excludes resolved domains, and adapts follow-up using actual completed/weak/incomplete findings. Failed fit assessments keep an explicit gap; they do not veto healthy follow-up. Existing evidence-only fit retry remains available.
- Companies and Research review provide reversible Good customer fit / Poor customer fit / Competitor or supplier / Wrong market / Unrelated business preferences. Advanced research tools lists all current-context preferences so undo remains available after clearing results. Feedback is bounded, normalized, saved in the workspace discovery section and scoped to seller domain, confirmed offer, ICP, exclusions and markets. It affects acquisition, never scores or qualification. Updating feedback is disabled during a running search.
- Automatic discovery reads the same scoped feedback, screens extracted names, preserves geographic targeting and avoids re-verifying previously checked domains. Seller website identity is now consistent across automatic company and buyer stages. The workspace Firecrawl proxy forwards validated geographic settings rather than silently dropping them.
- In-flight offer changes cancel stale work and restore a retryable state in the same workspace. A different workspace receives no late writes. Existing checked companies, actual scores, selected buyers, 80+ automatic-company gating and email-readiness policy are preserved.

## Independent review

The independent whole-branch review found four important issues, reproduced in failing regressions and corrected: full-name attribution masks overlapping longer identities before attributing an excerpt; active-ICP exclusion projection is idempotent so feedback context keys survive repeated normalization; signal queries use a separate family ordinal and retain weight order; and the browser runner allows a confirmed operational profile with no signals, references or known targets. The original strict-mode consumer also retains its lookalike boolean; balanced-mode regression now checks all three approved routes instead of the former fixed 60/40 split. No critical findings, declined judgments or deferred minor findings were reported.
