# Public company contact research

## Problem and benchmark

The Wernedal reference report found seven named company contacts, its registered CEO, six staff-page phone listings (Arad's is also the company switchboard), two named work emails and a general company email. The previously verified application retained four people and four phones. It missed leadership in registry tables and filtered non-buyer staff before their identities and published contact details could become durable company records.

## Required behavior for buyer searches

Acquire company contacts before ranking the buying committee. Keep additional named company contacts in a separately labelled record; an available email must not create purchasing authority or buyer suitability. Preserve actual titles, source URLs, unknown publication dates, email ownership and explicit gaps. Retain source data through workspace normalization and dedicated CRM records/snapshots. No paid email or phone reveal is authorized by identity discovery.

Official page acquisition first attempts authenticated, bounded public HTML/XML extraction without provider credentials. Preserve headings, bold staff names, links, and table boundaries. Stop on private-network destinations, unsafe redirects, unsupported bodies and streamed size limits. Reject blocker content. A configured Scrapling service can extract after a failed direct read; customer Firecrawl remains an extraction option. Existing service calls without public-first mode retain their customer-key requirements. Public-page audit events identify the actual extractor and zero extraction-provider credits; they do not claim AI or hosting is free.

Buyer crawls checkpoint each acquired page, prioritize contact/team/leadership/about pages and discovered sitemaps, use a 35-second initial budget and at most eight pages. Pages acquired for identity discovery are reused during the contact pass. The wider company record is bounded to sixty contacts and raw source bodies are held only in the current research run, rather than duplicated in the persisted company record.

Apollo company identity discovery starts before broad optional searches. It uses a domain-wide query without mandatory management/title filters and a company-name keyword fallback for sparse results. The fallback still retains the target domain in the application request for scoping/audit; every returned identity must match the target employer before retention. The API endpoint remains identity-only and paid reveal policies are unchanged.

The first public search wave is reduced from eighteen queries to six, including a leadership/registry query. Up to eight adaptive missing-function queries remain. Failed credential/quota responses switch the current run away from that Firecrawl key to the configured grounded web-search route. Identical per-run queries are reused; cancellation never starts another provider. Search summaries are not promoted to identity/contact evidence: underlying official/registry source pages must be read. Actual fallback providers are labelled in research coverage.

Official staff extraction retains administrative, logistics, professional-service and technical contacts in addition to buyer roles. Registry extraction accepts current executive role/name records only on a known registry or explicitly typed registry source with an exact company heading/title and a freshly read body. Auditors/board-only names, other employers and former-role sections are excluded. Birth-year annotations are removed, not persisted. Registry role evidence does not establish purchasing authority, email ownership or mailbox delivery.

## Verification

New regressions cover the seven-contact reference, two exactly published named work emails, CEO identity without fabricated email/phone, all company contacts in normalized workspace and CRM snapshots, separate shortlist selection, unrelated registries, renamed companies/domains, multiple industries, public extraction without a Firecrawl key, direct HTML staff formatting, blocker fallback, broad Apollo requests, fallback credential handling, valid empty responses versus invalid payloads, early checkpoints, provider ordering and preserved attribution boundaries.

A direct public-page read of Wernedal's actual staff page and Allabolag's current company officer page reproduced seven contacts, both published named work emails and all six staff phone listings. Live acceptance must additionally verify the deployed UI, CRM save, workspace sync/reload and exact release proof. External source availability and unpublished addresses remain explicit limits; this standard does not invent contacts to meet a count.

## Upgrade acceptance correction

The authenticated deployment test exposed an existing scheduler branch that reran an entire saved buyer search solely because the research version changed. Schema upgrades now leave the saved research intact and use the explicit Refresh buyers action for re-research. The existing single public check for newly unresolved contacts remains. A runtime regression verifies that opening an older saved dossier makes zero provider calls and retains its people, while a new unresolved identity receives one check.

### Whole-record parser acceptance correction
Authenticated acceptance exposed two service-page headings in the whole-company record. The broad staff path now consumes only extracted named staff claims, never raw page/result titles. Company-record entries retain validated provenance (official staff, public identity, exact-employer directory, or registry role); unvalidated records from the initial release are discarded during normalization/merge and reacquired from source, while saved buyer cards remain intact. A regression combines homepage/CAD service titles, staff, registry and stale records and requires exactly seven sourced people with two published emails after normalization/CRM mapping.

### CRM remediation
The first authenticated run had persisted two invalid page-title contacts before the parser correction. Exposed the existing workspace-scoped soft-archive endpoint through writer-only CRM contact actions, with in-app confirmation and company/workspace guards across the asynchronous confirmation. Archiving retains company and activity history, and archived identities cannot silently reappear. Regression covers action visibility, exact contact targeting, cancel and workspace switch.
