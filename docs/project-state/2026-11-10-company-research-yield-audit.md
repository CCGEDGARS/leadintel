# Company research yield: audit and acceptance contract

Outcome: build a relevant source-backed prospect shortlist for the active seller and market. Required release tests, an exact-SHA production proof and honest labels are necessary but do not establish useful customer results. Lower-score manual companies remain available; a normal discovery search must explicitly state whether its goal is strong fit (80+) or completed checks.

## Observed aggregate evidence

Read-only aggregate audit of the approved saved workspace found 124 search requests and 76 page reads against a 200-call allowance. Detailed per-call receipts were not recorded, so their exact stage split, retries and duplicate charges cannot be reconstructed. The last-pass website counter was nine; 30 checked domains were retained across saved work. Comparing the 200-call whole-request total directly to nine last-pass websites was incorrect. Do not turn that comparison into a historical efficiency claim.

The saved pool had 31 records: six in the saved checked array and 25 in research review, but only two passed the current live checks. Twenty lacked target-market presence; 13 lacked a currently verified purchasing application; five stored purchasing assessments used an older targeting context. These are aggregate observations, not evidence that every missing fact requires another website crawl. Candidate identities, raw source bodies, private prompts and credentials were not exported.

## Code causes to reproduce

- Official-site verification always searches recent buying-event news, even when the missing fact is static operating/location evidence.
- Verification starts a homepage crawl before knowing whether the search returned a readable official operating page, and checks a saved company online before reassessing its fresh saved evidence.
- Broad acquisition resolves/verifies a large company pool before evaluating fit and stopping on the actual goal.
- Automatic passes reset per-pass counters while retaining whole-request spending. A detailed bounded operation ledger is absent.
- The amount selector targets checked companies, allowing weak/adjacent manual checks to satisfy what the owner expects to be a strong-fit prospecting goal.

## Repair boundary and preservation

Reuse the existing evidence/qualification engine, saved review queue, workspace ownership and call caps. Do not weaken identity, market, operating-quote, exclusions or automatic-readiness gates. Do not rewrite approved messages, change contact-provider permissions, buy contact data, activate monitoring or send outreach. Preserve selected buyers, CRM, market evidence/reports and manual work. Reassess old-context commercial judgments using the configured AI and supplied current evidence; never bless them by merely relabelling the old context.

Implement evidence-first gap planning, market/operations-focused verification, search-result page reuse, progressively completed candidate batches, one request-scoped cache/operation ledger, consistent whole-request progress, and an explicit strong-fit goal for normal prospect discovery. Retain checked/manual compatibility for deliberate target/recheck and campaign paths.

Acceptance: failing-before/passing-after tests for each identified acquisition defect; budget, cancellation, scope replacement, save/reload and cross-seller/industry cases; real authenticated SQL/browser acceptance for the connected controls and usage display; no new full-budget research for deterministic QA. A final bounded live validation may use saved evidence first and only the remaining targeted gaps; report actual valid results and actual recorded operations separately from code/deployment status. A target is never a guarantee or a reason to invent evidence.

## Controlled verification recorded before release

The saved-evidence case reproduced three web requests before the fix and now uses zero web requests with one current-context AI reassessment. A readable official search result previously triggered a homepage crawl; two concurrent identical checks now share one search and preserve the original read timestamp. A four-company-batch acquisition fixture reaches five strong-fit companies within fifty requests rather than checking the entire pool first. These are controlled cases, not a measured Ercon-wide conversion or cost claim.

Cancellation testing also exposed same-millisecond request-ID collisions and orphaned in-flight cache joins. A monotonically increasing request sequence protects the ledger; abort releases the pending key and late responses cannot populate a new request's cache or receipts. Reassessment uses actual retained verification dates, never the reassessment clock as proof of a new page read.

The authenticated SQL/browser fixture verifies mixed 81/69 scores, a truthful one-of-five strong-fit shortfall, the preserved 22-company queue, a saved 20-request cap, sanitized receipt persistence and re-opened usage rendering. Desktop and mobile screenshots are retained by the existing CI browser job. Actual customer yield and production proof remain separate acceptance gates.
